Website privacy
and your choices.

Updated September 22, 2026

This page describes the VaultTail website at vaulttail.com. The Windows app has its own app privacy policy. Visiting this website does not give it access to the folders or USB drives on your computer.

Hosting and basic traffic statistics

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website and processes connection information, such as IP addresses and request details, to serve and protect it. Cloudflare Web Analytics may also provide basic traffic and page performance statistics. These hosting services are separate from Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, which are disabled on this website. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Website analytics are disabled

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity are disabled on this website. We do not load their tracking scripts or send them new page views, interaction events, or session recordings. No analytics consent banner is displayed.

Previous analytics choices

If you previously allowed analytics, the website removes its saved analytics choice and accessible Google Analytics and Clarity cookies when you next visit. This does not delete data previously sent to those providers or cookies on other domains. You can also clear this website’s data in your browser settings.

Support and external links

If you email us, we receive the address and information you include so we can respond. Please do not send passwords or private backup files. Microsoft Store and other linked services have their own privacy practices.

Contact

For questions about this website or its data handling, email support.vaulttail@gmail.com.
